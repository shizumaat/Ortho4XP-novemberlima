- The big movers are the **decompose / split / sliver-merge / drop** family and
  the **reclassify** family — these are the cohesive submodules M7b should form
  (`absorb`, `drop`, `reclassify`, `vertex_fix`, `split`). `_reclassify_*` is a
  clean 3-pass family; `_drop_*` is a 3-pass family (two no-op here);
  `_merge_sliver` + `_drop_thin_orphan` are the sliver cleanup.
- `_reclassify_apron_junctions` is a pure ROLE flip (apron↔junction, 82 over the
  set) with zero geometry change — cheap, load-bearing.
- The 4 no-op-here passes and 5 never-called passes are the consolidation
  candidates, but **confirm on a wider fixture set first** (this is 4 airports).
- Tool caveat: passes are matched by `layout`-first-parameter and by identity
  across all loaded `auto_patch` modules (so aliased imports like `_awj` are
  caught); pipeline must be imported before `junction_repair` (the
  junction_repair↔elevation cycle, CLAUDE.md).
